Our Story

Why We Built ClearCut

We were frustrated that every good background remover required an account, an internet connection, charged a subscription, or stamped a watermark on your photos. ClearCut was the solution we needed for ourselves—and now we're sharing it free with the world.

🎯

The Mission

Make professional-grade background removal free and accessible for every Android user on the planet—no gatekeeping, no subscriptions.

🛡️

The Privacy Promise

We believe your photos are private. That is why ClearCut uses on-device AI. Your images never leave your phone, ever.

⚡

Speed First

Removing a background should take under a second. Our neural network is optimized specifically for mobile hardware.

🌍

Built for Everyone

Works without internet in remote areas, on flights, in hospitals. Designed for users in all connectivity environments.
