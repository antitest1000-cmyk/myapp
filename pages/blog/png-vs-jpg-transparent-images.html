PNG vs JPG: Which Is Better for Transparent Images?

The short answer: use PNG for transparent backgrounds, always. But the full picture is more nuanced—here's everything you need to know.

PNG – Portable Network Graphics

Supports full alpha channel (transparency)
Lossless compression (no quality degradation)
Best for logos, cutouts, illustrations
Larger file size than JPEG
Slower to load on very large files

JPEG / JPG – Joint Photographic Group

Does NOT support transparency
Lossy compression (some quality loss)
Much smaller file sizes for photos
Best for photographs with solid backgrounds
Universally supported everywhere

Why JPEG Cannot Be Transparent

JPEG was designed in 1992 for compressing photographs. Its format specification does not include an alpha channel (the data layer that stores per-pixel transparency). When a JPEG is saved, any transparency information is simply discarded and replaced with white or black. This is why you'll sometimes see "broken" transparent images displayed with a white background—someone saved a PNG as JPEG.

PNG: The Right Choice for Cutouts

PNG (Portable Network Graphics) was designed specifically to support transparency via an alpha channel—an additional layer alongside Red, Green, and Blue that stores 0–255 opacity for each pixel. ClearCut automatically exports PNG format when you choose to keep the background transparent.

When to Use JPEG Instead

If you've applied a solid color or photo background to your ClearCut result, there's no transparency to preserve—so JPEG is perfectly fine and produces a smaller, faster file. ClearCut exports JPEG when a background is applied. For social media posts or email attachments with solid backgrounds, JPEG is the practical choice.

File Size Comparison

A 12MP cutout with transparency as PNG: ~4–8MB. The same image as JPEG (solid white background, 85% quality): ~2–3MB. WebP (a newer format supported in Android): ~1.5–2MB with alpha support. ClearCut currently exports PNG and JPEG. WebP export is planned for a future update.

Which Format Does ClearCut Export?

ClearCut intelligently selects the format based on your background choice. Transparent background → PNG (alpha preserved). Solid color or photo background → JPEG (smaller file, no transparency needed). You can also manually select the export format in Settings → Output Format.
